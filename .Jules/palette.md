## 2026-08-05 - [A11y & Focus States for Mobile Bottom Navigation]
**Learning:** For bottom navigation elements on touch and mobile-responsive screens, visual focus outlines are often omitted, breaking keyboard navigability when viewing under emulation or desktop-responsive viewports. Including standard focus rings paired with descriptive `aria-label` attributes and `aria-current="page"` state indicators elevates accessibility to WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Marking purely decoration sub-elements (icons, text) with `aria-hidden="true"` prevents screen-readers from reading redundant text.
**Action:** When working with bottom navigation bars, enforce keyboard focus indicators, mark current navigation items with `aria-current="page"`, and wrap active/inactive anchors in concise, screen-reader-friendly `aria-label` tags.
